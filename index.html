Real-time voice streaming gateway for AI voice bots

A WebSocket gateway that connects live Asterisk phone calls to AI voice bots. Caller audio streams to speech-to-text, the transcript goes to an LLM, and the reply streams back through text-to-speech to the caller, all while the call is live. It now handles 100K+ calls a day, peaking at 200K. I also helped integrate Groq for LLM inference and ElevenLabs for speech.
